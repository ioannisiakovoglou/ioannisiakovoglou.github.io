These are the toughts of a romantic idealist, who admires Plato a bit too much, for he freed ideas from the physical world and placed them in the same space as ideals. ***The mathematical world*** Welcome to the mathematical world! For those among you that have never been here before, know that this world appears to humans as if it was governed by fog, but despite the fog that surrounds it, mathematicians have created for us maps and shelters, thanks to which we can fortunately travel. It is only thanks to those that one is able to meet many of the inhabitants of the maths world and to navigate efficiently through the difficult weather conditions or the snowy mountains. What a huge and beautiful world this is and how numerous are the directions in which nobody has ever traveled before. ***Mathematicians as part of the mathematical world*** Mathematicians are explorers and navigators of the mathematical world. They improve the maps of their predecessors, while at the same time they map new previously unknown regions of this world. Some dedicate their work in the study of the inner workings of the maths world, while others are more interested in its inhabitants, their mutual relations or even their classification. ***What we know so far*** Despite the collective of efforts of several generations of mathematicians, not much is known about the maths world. However, several useful observations have been made 1. The mathematical world is composed by a finite collection of atoms, known as axioms, linked together by logical bonds. 2. Every object inside the math world seems boundaryless, as its relation with other objects needs to be carefully examined every time. Even more, every time someone tries to shed more light to the region that seemingly contains the boundary of an object, one only discovers a much more intricate form that seems to form the boundary separating the object from its neighbors. 3. The mathematical world seems to be connected with our physical world. It is as if it contained moe than the entirety of our physical world, thus giving us ways to predict the future, improve our technology and avoid natural catastrophies. 4. Among the inhabitants of the Maths world, there are renowned beasts, famous for their numerous applications and mythological beauty. Those have only been seen by legendary mathematicians. Only few of them have ever been documented. ***About Mathematical flowers*** Occasionally, while I wonder through the mathematical world, looking for a new object to document, I cross fields filled with flowers. Altough flowers carry much less interest than a beast, it has come to my attention that we mathematicians have become completely unimpressed by their existence. We just step on them, as a means to reach faster new heights, while the knowledge of their existence remains solely ours. What a shame. ***My Identity as a Mathematician*** I do not consider myself to be a mathematical bounty hunter. Although I appreciate very much thinking and dreaming about beasts, it is the elagance and the beauty of the beast that drives me and much less the price of its head. ***About artificial intelligence*** Fellow travellers a mathematical super robot has suddenly appeared in the maths world! You can now look for beasts from a distance by only giving to the machine a general description. You only need to press the button. This new super machine can climb mountains with incredible speeds, can cross fields and caves so much more efficiently and then reports back to you all the beasts that it has encountered in its passage. You may now collect prizes and recognition so much faster, just press the button. No need to exhaust the body anymore, no need to crawl in the mud, or climb those snowy mountains. Rejoice, for humans have prevailed! I mean... you still need to press that button. I only hope that the ones after me will still be able to experience fellowship and the beauty of the journey in the same fulfilling way as I did.
