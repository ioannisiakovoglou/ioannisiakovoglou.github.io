--- layout: archive permalink: /year-archive/ title: "Some thoughts" author_profile: true redirect_from: - /wordpress/blog-posts/ --- ***How I picture a mathematician*** From my perspective, mathematicians are explorers and navigators of the world of Mathematics: a world composed by a finite set of atoms, known as axioms, linked together by logical bonds. It is their duty to spend time mapping the mathematical world, in order to uncover its inner workings, study its boundaryless inhabitants, whose mutual relations are mostly unknown to us, and visit courageously new previously unknown areas. Mathematicians form a bridge between the physical and mathematical worlds. They assign words to mathematical ideas, they extract from them usefulness and beauty, which are used to generate applications, art and new ways of thinking. ***About Mathematical flowers*** Among the inhabitants of the Maths world, there are renowned beasts, famous for their numerous applications and mythological beauty. One may also find steep paths leading to heigher peaks of understanding or even tools for more efficient navigation. However, it is my opinion that we do not talk enough about the flowers. Instead, we step on them while chasing rare beasts, while we are the only ones aware of their existence. It is such a shame that this knowledge is mostly reserved to mathematicians. ***My Identity as a Mathematician*** I do not consider myself to be a mathematical bounty hunter. Although I appreciate very much thinking and dreaming about beasts, it is the elagance and the beauty of the beast that drives me and much less the price of its head. ***About artificial intelligence*** Fellow travellers a mathematical super robot has suddenly appeared! You can now kill beasts from a distance without even knowing what the beast looks like. Just press the button. This new super machine can climb mountains with incredible speeds and reports back to you all the beasts that it has encountered or that it has slain in its passage. You only need to press the button. No need to exhaust the body anymore, no need to crawl in the mud, or climb those snowy mountains. Rejoice, for humans have prevailed! I mean... you still need to press that button. I only hope that the ones after me will still be able to experience fellowship and the beauty of the journey in the same fulfilling way as me.
