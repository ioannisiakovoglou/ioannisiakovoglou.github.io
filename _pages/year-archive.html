--- layout: archive permalink: /year-archive/ title: "Some thoughts" author_profile: true redirect_from: - /wordpress/blog-posts/ --- ***How I picture a mathematician*** From my perspective, mathematicians are explorers and navigators of the world of Mathematics: a world defined by a set of elementary axioms and a list of logical rules. The more one observes the Maths world, the more they become aware of its immensness, uncover its inner workings, encounter several of its boundaryless inhabitants, whose mutual relations are mostly unknown to us, and visit new areas, some of which have been previously conquered by others and some which are completely new to all of us. Mathematicians form a bridge between the physical and mathematical worlds. They assign words to mathematical ideas, they extract from them usefulness and beauty, which are used to generate applications, art and new ways of thinking. ***On Mathematical flowers*** Among the inhabitants of the Maths world, there are renowned beasts, famous for their numerous applications and mythological beauty. One may also find steep paths leading to heigher peaks of understanding or even tools for more efficient navigation. However, it is my opinion that we do not talk enough about the flowers. Instead, we step on them while chasing rare beasts, while we are the only ones aware of their existence. It is such a shame that this knowledge is mostly reserved to mathematicians. ***My Identity as a Mathematician*** I do not identify myself as a mathematical bounty hunter. Although I appreciate very much thinking and dreaming about beasts, it is the elagance and the beauty of the beast that drives me and much less the price of its head.
