This part of my website has been created for purely artistic purposes. These are the thoughts and feelings of a romantic idealist, who admires Plato a bit too much, for he freed ideas from the physical world and placed them in the same space as ideals.

The mathematical world

Welcome to the mathematical world! For those among you who have never been here before, know that this world appears to humans as if it were governed by fog. Yet, despite the fog that surrounds it, mathematicians have created maps and shelters, thanks to which we can fortunately travel.

It is only thanks to these that one is able to meet many of the inhabitants of the maths world and navigate efficiently through difficult weather conditions and snowy mountains.

What a huge and beautiful world this is, and how numerous are the directions in which nobody has ever travelled before!

Researchers as part of the mathematical world

Researchers in Mathematics are explorers and navigators of the mathematical world. They improve the maps of their predecessors, while at the same time mapping previously unknown regions of this world.

Some dedicate their work to the study of the inner workings of the maths world, while others are more interested in its inhabitants, their existence or inexistence, their mutual relations or even the classification of their species.

What we know so far

Despite the collective efforts of several generations of mathematicians, not much is known about the maths world. However, several useful observations have been made:

  1. The mathematical world is composed of a finite collection of atoms, known as axioms, linked together by logical bonds.
  2. Every object inside the maths world seems boundaryless, as its relation with other objects needs to be carefully examined every time. Even more, whenever someone tries to shed more light on the region that seemingly contains the boundary of an object, one discovers a much more intricate form that appears to separate the object from its neighbours.
  3. The mathematical world seems to be connected with our physical world. It is as if it contained more than the entirety of our physical world, thus giving us ways to predict the future, improve our technology and avoid natural catastrophes.
  4. Among the inhabitants of the maths world, there are renowned beasts, famous for their numerous applications and mythological beauty. These have only been seen by legendary mathematicians. Only a few of them have ever been documented.

About mathematical flowers

Occasionally, while I wander through the mathematical world, looking for a new object to document, I cross fields filled with flowers.

Although flowers carry much less interest than a beast, it has come to my attention that we mathematicians have become completely unimpressed by their existence. We just step on them as a means of reaching new heights faster, while the knowledge of their existence remains solely ours.

What a shame.

My identity as a mathematician

I do not consider myself to be a mathematical bounty hunter. Although I very much appreciate thinking and dreaming about beasts, it is the elegance and beauty of the beast that drives me, much less than the price of its head.

About artificial intelligence

Fellow travellers, a mathematical super robot has suddenly appeared in the maths world!

You can now look for beasts from a distance simply by giving the machine a general description. You only need to press the button.

This new super machine can climb mountains at incredible speeds, cross fields and caves so much more efficiently, and then report back all the beasts that it has encountered along the way.

You may now collect prizes and recognition so much faster. Just press the button.

No need to exhaust the body anymore, no need to crawl in the mud or climb those snowy mountains. Rejoice, for humans have prevailed! I mean... you still need to press that button.

I only hope that those who come after me will still be able to experience fellowship and the beauty of the journey in the same fulfilling way as I did.
